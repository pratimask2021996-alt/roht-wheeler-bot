Hardware Interconnections (How Every Component is Connected)

To successfully bring the Obstacle Avoiding Bot.MIS to life, physical wiring must perfectly align with embedded hardware interfaces.

  • The Sensor Circuitry: The HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor requires a steady 5V power supply from the Arduino rail. Its Trigger and Echo pins are routed to analog interfaces A0 and A1 to ensure real-time distance polling without lag.
  • The Servo Mechanism: The SG90 micro servo, which swivels our ultrasonic radar, has its signal line mapped directly to digital PWM pin 10 for high-precision angular movements.
  • The Propulsion Core: The L298N H-Bridge Motor Driver acts as the high-current muscles of the robot. Its directional input pins (IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4) are safely connected to Arduino digital pins 4, 5, 6, and 7 to execute clean forward, reverse, and spot-turning commands.
