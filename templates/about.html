In-Depth Project Overview (Every Nook and Corner)

The Obstacle Avoiding Bot.MIS is an advanced engineering prototype designed to simulate real-world autonomous navigation. The core philosophy of this project is to create an enclosed robotic system capable of spatial awareness, obstacle categorization, threat mitigation, and self-correction without relying on pre-mapped terrain or external telemetry.

Every single inch of this robot serves a critical function:

  • The Chassis Geometry: Designed using a lightweight high-grade acrylic dual-deck structure to lower the center of gravity. This ensures maximum balance when the robot takes sharp zero-turn pivots to avoid obstacles.
  • The Propulsion Sub-system: Utilizing two independent, high-torque plastic-geared BO motors operating on a 1:48 reduction ratio. This specific gearing provides optimal torque over raw speed, allowing the robot to come to an immediate emergency halt the millisecond an object is detected.
  • The Power Distribution Network: To prevent electrical noise spikes (generated by the DC motors) from crashing or resetting the delicate Arduino microchip, we implemented a dual-power rail design. The logic board runs on clean regulated current, while the motor driver pulls high-amperage raw power directly from a lithium-ion cell arrangement.
  • The Feedback Cycle: The system works on a relentless 3-step loop: Sense (Ultrasonic radar scans space) → Process (Arduino analyzes the distance parameters) → Act (Motor driver changes wheel velocity).
