The Context of the Project

The fields of robotics, embedded systems, and automation are expanding at an unprecedented rate, changing how we interact with technology daily. The development of Obstacle Avoiding Bot.MIS originated from a simple yet profound question: How can we design an intelligent system that safely navigates unknown physical spaces using minimal hardware?

This project is an academic effort to dive deep into the fundamentals of autonomous machine navigation. It serves as a practical milestone in our educational journey, pulling core concepts directly out of theoretical textbooks and transforming them into a real-world working engineering prototype.

// Section 2

Personal Motivation & Learning Journey

Our primary motivation was to understand the intricate synergy between hardware interfaces and embedded computing blocks. Throughout the development lifecycle of this project, we faced and resolved several classic engineering bottlenecks—ranging from sensor noise mitigation and ground chassis calibration to dual-rail power isolation. Overcoming these hurdles has dramatically enhanced our problem-solving skills and deepened our core competencies in electronic design and C++ syntax architecture.
